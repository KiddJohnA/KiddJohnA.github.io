The Encyclopedia of Real Computation

An encyclopedia figured with one computation: every subject run through the same twenty-two terms — Principle, Purpose, all the way to Answer — in plain English, with the work shown. Eighteen volumes: the Book of Knowledge, one volume for each of the sixteen fields, and the Sum of All Knowledge.
