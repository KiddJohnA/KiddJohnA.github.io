The problem

Two walls.

Computing is running into the same two walls from both sides — one in the machine, one in the mind.

The heat wall

Today’s chips move electrons through wires, and electrons make heat. Every generation pushes more power through smaller spaces, and more of that power is wasted warming the room. Light doesn’t have this problem — photons carry information without the friction.

The thinking wall

Knowledge is scattered across books, notes, and half-finished ideas, with no common way to figure one thing from another. People have the pieces; what’s missing is a machine for putting a term in and getting the figured answer out.

Chron.Codex · Five modes

The Codex takes any term — coffee, music, cooking, a business, a machine — and works it through the Unified Equation: a fixed grammar of twenty-two variables that turns a word into a full computation, with the work shown.

Five modes put the same engine to work: Figure computes a term, Plan lays out the steps, Design shapes the thing, Ask answers from the figure, and Solve turns the figure loose on a problem. Every readout lands on a shareable statement card.

The wider shelf.

Every future line gets the same treatment as the first two: the full computation, the real-world numbers, the parts, and the business — written down before a dollar is spent building it.

Atomic Batteries

Document complete · Oct 2026

Power cells that run for decades on radioactive decay — a carbon-14 core sealed in diamond, inside a second casing. The figuring checks out against the real physics: total energy scales with the mass loaded, and power per gram runs inverse to the half-life. The document covers the isotope table, the five parts, the costs, and the honest limits of what today’s cells can actually put out. Chron’s plan: sell cells, not isotope — tritium first, defence and remote sensors first.

Spacetime Gates

Research line

Chron’s research program on closed timelike curves (CTCs) — the physics of gates through spacetime, figured honestly: the simulations say holding a gate open takes negative energy nobody yet knows how to make. The figuring waits, ready, for the breakthrough.
